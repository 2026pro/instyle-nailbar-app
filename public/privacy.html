Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 3, 2026 · Applies to the InStyle OS app and os.instylebl.com

InStyle Beauty Lounge ("InStyle", "we", "us") operates the InStyle OS application (web, Android, and iOS) used by our clients to book appointments and by our staff to manage the salon. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Clients: name, email address, and phone number, provided by you when you register or request a booking; your appointment history with our salon.
  • Staff: work account details, schedules, time-clock entries, and service/earnings records needed to run the salon.
  • We do not collect payment card numbers in the app. Card payments are processed at the salon by our payment terminal provider (Helcim); we store only the transaction amount and payment type.
  • We do not use advertising trackers and we do not collect your precise location.

How we use it

  • To create and manage your appointments and account.
  • To send booking confirmations and appointment reminders by email (including optional Google Calendar invitations). We never robocall you.
  • With your consent, we may send occasional offers or promotions by text message to the phone number you provided. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP or contacting us.
  • To operate the salon: schedules, payroll, and service records for our staff.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers that run the app for us — our database and hosting providers (Supabase, Vercel, Cloudflare) and our email delivery provider — and only as needed to provide the service, or when required by law.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your information while your account or booking history is active. You may ask us to delete your account and personal information at any time — email or call us using the contact details below and we will remove it within 30 days.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and stored with access controls. No method of storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Children

InStyle OS is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
